Building Champions On and Off the Court

We are passionate about building a thriving volleyball community in the East Bay area.

Our Mission

As a new volleyball club located on the east side of California's Bay Area, we serve both boys' and girls' teams. Although the club will have the majority of our practices in Brentwood, we are here to support all of our surrounding areas.

Our overarching mission is to foster a fun, inclusive, and engaging environment. We believe in providing a space for individuals and entire families to come together and truly enjoy the game of volleyball.

A Legacy of Growth and Success

Striving to offer the best possible experience for our members.

East Bay Volleyball Club was founded with the vision of creating a space where volleyball enthusiasts of all ages and skill levels could come together. Although we are just starting, we are already making waves in the local volleyball scene.

Our commitment to excellence and community engagement sets us apart. We are dedicated to continuous improvement and growth, always striving to offer the best possible experience for our members.
